Architectural Wallcovering Services

From fragile historic hand-painted silks to 54-inch heavy-duty commercial vinyl, we execute every wall project with surgical precision and white-glove respect.

01 • RESIDENTIAL EXCELLENCE

Luxury Designer Wallpaper Installation

High-end residential wallcoverings demand far more than basic application. Designer brands such as Cole & Son, Phillip Jeffries, Scalamandré, Sanderson, and Pierre Frey feature intricate repeat registrations and delicate finishes that allow zero room for error.

Our certified paperhangers ensure exact plumb-line alignment, flawless wrap around internal and external corners, switch plate camouflage, and zero visible seam lines.

  • ✦ Straight, Half-Drop & Random Match calculation
  • ✦ Ceiling & alcove wallpapering with precision trim
  • ✦ Hand-trimmed salvage edges for seamless butt-joints
  • ✦ Archival wheat and clay paste formulations matched to substrate
Request Residential Quote
02 • SCENIC FINE ART

Scenic Murals & Custom Panoramic Panels

Scenic murals and chinoiserie panels transform a blank room into an immersive landscape. Because each drop contains unique non-repeating artwork, careful pre-layout and elevation planning are essential to avoid cutting through focal motifs around doors, windows, or sconces.

We dry-sequence every panel on site, verify wall elevations against manufacturer schematics, and double-cut through seams with fresh surgical carbon blades for undetectable panel transitions.

  • ✦ Custom scale verification and architectural focal centering
  • ✦ Hand-painted panoramic silks (Gracie, de Gournay, Fromental)
  • ✦ Double-cut splicing technique for continuous scenery
  • ✦ Protective topcoat sealing for high-humidity areas
Inquire Scenic Mural Project
03 • ORGANIC TEXTURES

Grasscloth, Raffia & Specialty Silks

Handwoven natural fibers—such as Japanese sisal, arrowroot, raw hemp, paperweave, and textured jute—require artisan sensitivity. Natural materials celebrate organic color variances ("shading") that must be balanced artistically across the room.

Because glue touching the front face of natural grasscloth permanently stains the fibers, we utilize dry-hanging and back-pasting techniques with zero squeeze-out.

  • ✦ Pre-installation roll shading balance and sequence grading
  • ✦ Zero-paste-face technique to preserve fragile natural fibers
  • ✦ Wood veneered papers & cork metallic laminates
  • ✦ Seamless acoustic textile wrapping
Discuss Grasscloth Project

Commercial & Hospitality Installations

High-traffic commercial corridors, luxury retail flagship boutiques, private dining clubs, and executive suites need wallcoverings that combine arresting aesthetics with industrial durability and fire-rating compliance.

We specialize in 54-inch wide Type II heavy-duty commercial vinyl, Osnaburg and scrim-backed wallcoverings, Class A fire-rated installations, and sound-absorbing acoustic wall treatments.

  • ✦ 54-inch commercial vinyl hanging with table pasting machines
  • ✦ Acoustic baffling & felt fabric wall systems

05 • ARCHITECTURAL PREPARATION

Wallpaper Stripping & Surface Restoration

Even the most magnificent wallcovering will fail if installed over an unprimed, uneven, or degraded wall. Before hanging any new material, our master restoration team restores the underlying drywall or historic plaster to glass-smooth perfection.

If you have outdated existing wallpaper, we gently strip old layers without shredding sheetrock paper, wash away residual glue, skim coat the substrate, and apply pigmented acrylic wall size.

  • ✦ Chemical-free, low-moisture steam removal
  • ✦ Level 5 skim coating & dustless sanding
  • ✦ Mold, mildew, and moisture barrier priming (Gardz / Roman Pro)
  • ✦ Guaranteed future damage-free strippability

STANDARDS OF THE GUILD

Our 5-Stage Precision Process

A disciplined methodology that guarantees exact pattern registration, clean job sites, and timeless durability.

01

Laser Diagnostics

We measure wall elevations with optical laser levels and test drywall moisture and relative humidity before unrolling materials.

02

Substrate Priming

Old paste is dissolved, flaws are skimmed, and an impenetrable acrylic barrier size is applied to ensure adhesion and future strippability.

03

Drop Sequence

Rolls are unboxed, inspected for dye-lot batch consistency, trimmed of salvage edges, and staged in sequence around the room focal point.

04

Laser Hanging

Using laser plumb guides and substrate-matched adhesive chemistry, each strip is hung with hand-rolled seams and zero paste face contact.

05

High-CRI Audit

Every corner, seam, and baseboard trim line is audited under 98+ CRI inspection lamps to verify zero bubbles, ripples, or seam shine.
