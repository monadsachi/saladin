FEATURED CASE STUDY

The Gold Coast Penthouse Scenic Dining Rotunda

The Challenge: A curved 360-degree dining rotunda in a 58th-floor penthouse required the continuous installation of an 18-panel French panoramic scenic landscape on delicate raw silk with varying radius curves and integrated pocket doors.

The Solution: Our team developed curved vertical laser projection guides, applied a clay-rich low-water adhesive formulated not to shrink during curing, and double-cut every panel joint in place with micro-blade changes between drops.

Client Verdict

"The transitions across the curved pocket doors are invisible. Saladin's craftsmen solved architectural complexities that two previous contractors declared impossible."
