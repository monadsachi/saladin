ARTISANAL MASTERY

Where Micrometers Matter: Precision Paperhanging

Installing luxury wallcoverings is a surgical craft. Unlike paint, patterned wallpapers allow zero forgiveness. A half-millimeter deviation on drop one translates into glaring pattern misalignment across the entire room.

We calibrate our drops using optical vertical lasers, roll every seam with custom hardwood tools to prevent glossing delicate inks, and formulate adhesive viscosity specifically to the paper backing.
